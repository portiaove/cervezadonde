The most important correction to the old instructions is that the product is
already **Spain-wide**. OSM is the national canonical POI source and four
official censos are enrichment layers. Madrid's ordinance is not a valid
country-wide policy, and existence, classification, hours, and freshness must
remain separate confidence dimensions.
